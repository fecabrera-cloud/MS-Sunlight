Figure 6. Microbial functional responses at the end of the microcosm experiment. Measurements were made after 51 days of incubation in darkness. Panels show (A) litter dry-mass loss, (B) SIR-derived microbial biomass C, (C) potential beta-glucosidase activity, and (D) the biomass-to-respiration ratio. Colors indicate microcosm substrate: full sunlight, yellow; reduced UV, light blue; and reduced UV and blue-green, dark grey. Shapes indicate inoculum origin: full sunlight, circles; reduced UV, triangles; and reduced UV and blue-green, squares. Colored points represent individual inoculated microcosms. White diamonds and error bars show model-estimated substrate marginal means, weighted equally across inoculum origins, and pointwise 95% confidence intervals. Biomass, enzyme activity, and the ratio were analyzed on the natural-log scale; their displayed estimates and intervals are back-transformed, and the estimates are geometric means. Full factorial mixed models included substrate, inoculum, and their interaction as fixed effects and experimental block as a random intercept. Corner labels summarize Type III Satterthwaite F tests: S, BH-adjusted q < 0.05; NS, q >= 0.05, with adjustment across the four measurements separately for each model term. Brackets show significant substrate contrasts, with Tukey-adjusted p-values for the family of three pairwise comparisons within each measurement. Dry-mass loss had a significant overall substrate test but no significant pairwise contrast. N = 46 for dry-mass loss, biomass, and enzyme activity; N = 43 for the biomass-to-respiration ratio. The ratio is a relative allocation index calculated from SIR-derived biomass C divided by integrated respiration and is not a direct physiological measurement of carbon-use efficiency.
